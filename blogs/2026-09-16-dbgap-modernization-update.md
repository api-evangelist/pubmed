NCBI is modernizing the database of Genotype and Phenotype (dbGaP), a repository that collects human research study data from NIH-funded investigators and facilitates controlled-access to individual-level data. As previously announced, we released a modernized homepage in 2025. Now, we’ve extended that modernization to the public search and study record pages.
